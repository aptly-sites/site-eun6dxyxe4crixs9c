About Norwood

Norwood is one of the most strategically located cities in the Cincinnati metro — surrounded entirely by Cincinnati and bisected by I-71, with quick access to downtown, the east side, and the suburbs. Founded in the late 19th century as a streetcar suburb, Norwood today combines its historic residential grid with the major Rookwood Commons and Rookwood Pavilion retail districts, plus the Surrey Square and Linden Pointe developments.

Housing is predominantly early 20th-century single-family — brick foursquares, bungalows, and two-family flats — with pockets of duplexes and apartment buildings on the cross streets. The walkable street grid and proximity to multiple commercial corridors keep Norwood consistently attractive to both owner-occupants and investors.

Strong rental demand from young professionals, healthcare workers, and corporate employees at nearby campuses (Xavier University, Procter & Gamble, Cincinnati Children's) makes Norwood one of the most reliable rental markets in the metro. Inventory turns quickly and vacancy stays low.

Frequently Asked Questions — Norwood

Norwood has a median household income of ~$58,000. Approximately 55% of units are rentals. Average rents currently range from around $1,600 for a one-bedroom to $2,650 for a three-bedroom. EquityTeam has managed properties in Norwood since 2008.

Property management fees in Norwood usually include:

• Monthly Management Fees: 8% to 12% of monthly rent.
• Leasing Fees: 50–100% of one month’s rent, sometimes a flat fee of around $800–$1,000.
• Lease Renewal Fees: from a flat fee of around $100 per renewal up to 6% of the total lease term’s cumulative gross rents.
• Maintenance: Some companies charge hourly rates (starting around $60–$70 per hour and up) for an internal technician, while others outsource all maintenance to local vendors and pass the vendor bill through to the owner with a slight markup. Typically, those with internal maintenance techs respond faster, do better work, and cost the owner less in the long run.

It can be hard to find these prices online since many management companies are not transparent with their pricing. EquityTeam publishes its pricing online on our Residential Property Management page.
