Dayton, Ohio

Dayton, Ohio, a jewel of the Midwest, has a rich history intertwined with innovation and cultural evolution. Known as the Birthplace of Aviation, Dayton has been home to visionaries like the Wright Brothers, who forever changed the course of human history.

Today, Dayton continues to be a hub of progress, boasting a thriving arts scene, diverse culinary landscape, and vibrant community spirit. From the historic Oregon District to the modern attractions of the Dayton Art Institute and the National Museum of the United States Air Force, there's something for everyone in this dynamic city.

Dayton's economy is bolstered by a mix of manufacturing, healthcare, and technology sectors, providing a stable and varied job market. With its blend of historical charm and modern amenities, Dayton offers an unparalleled quality of life that has attracted residents from across the nation.

Dayton Property Management FAQs

Dayton has a median household income of ~$45,000. Approximately 52% of units are rentals. Average rents currently range from around $850 for a one-bedroom to $1,500 for a three-bedroom. EquityTeam has managed properties in Dayton since 2008.

Property management fees in Dayton usually include:

• Monthly Management Fees: 8% to 12% of monthly rent.
• Leasing Fees: 50–100% of one month’s rent, sometimes a flat fee of around $800–$1,000.
• Lease Renewal Fees: from a flat fee of around $100 per renewal up to 6% of the total lease term’s cumulative gross rents.
• Maintenance: Some companies charge hourly rates (starting around $60–$70 per hour and up) for an internal technician, while others outsource all maintenance to local vendors and pass the vendor bill through to the owner with a slight markup. Typically, those with internal maintenance techs respond faster, do better work, and cost the owner less in the long run.

It can be hard to find these prices online since many management companies are not transparent with their pricing. EquityTeam publishes its pricing online on our Residential Property Management page.
