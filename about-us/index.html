Our Values

These values, based on the acronym “property” are ingrained in everything we do.

EquityTeam’s operating values spell the word PROPERTY — a reminder that every property we manage is the result of intentional standards, not accident. These eight principles guide how we manage residential rentals, vacation rentals, HOAs, commercial properties, and shared living communities across Greater Cincinnati, Greater Dayton, and Norris Lake, Tennessee.

People First

EquityTeam is built around the people who make property management work — property owners, tenants, vendors, and our internal team. Every process we operate in Cincinnati, Dayton, and Norris Lake, Tennessee is designed to help those relationships succeed long-term, not extract short-term transactions.

Rank Quality Over Quantity

We do not chase unit counts. EquityTeam focuses on managing residential rentals, vacation rentals, HOAs, commercial properties, and shared living communities at a quality standard that has earned us the highest-rated property management reputation in Cincinnati since 2015.

Over Deliver

We set realistic expectations with every property owner we serve and then work to exceed them. In 2025, both our Property Management and Property Services divisions met or exceeded every benchmark tracked by our third-party national KPI service — placing both service lines in the top 10% of property management companies nationally.

Professional

Professional property management means experience, accountability, continuous improvement, and clear communication. EquityTeam is an Ohio licensed real estate brokerage (REC.2012001994) and operates with the systems, technology, and standards expected of a top-tier management firm.

Ethical Excellence

Honesty, integrity, and the Golden Rule guide every decision EquityTeam makes — whether we are placing a tenant in a Cincinnati rental, managing an HOA board in Dayton, or operating a vacation rental at Norris Lake under our Deerfield Vacation Rentals brand. We expect the same standard from the owners, tenants, and vendors we work with.

Resourceful

Property management problems rarely fit a script. EquityTeam's team finds clever, cost-effective solutions to maintenance issues, tenant disputes, vacancy challenges, and regulatory questions — drawing on nearly two decades of operating across multiple Ohio and Tennessee markets.

T.E.A.M.

Great property management is a team sport. EquityTeam combines licensed property managers, in-house maintenance technicians, leasing specialists, inspectors, and operations staff so every property owner gets a coordinated team — not a single overworked point of contact.

Yield

Every decision EquityTeam makes is weighed against the return on investment it produces for property owners. Whether the question is rent pricing, a capital improvement, a vendor selection, or a vacancy strategy, we evaluate it through the lens of long-term yield — because protecting owner ROI is the entire point of professional property management.

Our Story

Learn what made us the company we are today, and what our goals are for the future.

2003

Rooted in Wholesale Acquisitions

CEO Mark Thompson started in Cincinnati real estate by mastering wholesale acquisitions — sourcing below-market deals for investors. The company scaled fast across services (acquisitions, realty, hard money lending, title) and markets (Cincinnati, Dayton, Columbus, Northern Kentucky), becoming a regional leader in residential investment services from 2004–2008.
2003
2008
2008

EquityTeam Founded — Full-Service Oriented

As the housing bubble reshaped the market, EquityTeam was founded as a full-service real estate investment firm built around client success, not transaction volume. We broadened beyond acquisitions and funding to add construction management and exit-strategy services/consulting.
2012

The Shift to Residential Property Management

For years we hired Cincinnati's "best" property managers — and were consistently let down. We learned that poor property management is one of the biggest threats to long-term investment success, yet no reliable partner existed in the market. So we reached the obvious conclusion: if you want it done right, do it yourself. We set out to build the property management company we wanted — and the one the market needed — grounded in industry best practices and a genuine win/win between owner and manager.
2012
2015
2015

A Cincinnati Market Leader

By 2015, EquityTeam earned the distinction of becoming the highest-rated residential property management company in Cincinnati, a reputation we proudly maintain.
2021

Expansion into Dayton, OH

Our first acquisition — a small Dayton property management company — extended our services into the neighboring Dayton, Ohio market, where we've retained 90% of the units.
2021
